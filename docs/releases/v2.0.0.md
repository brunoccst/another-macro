Another Macro 2.0: a new app built on the generic macro-forge core, with the Statistics page, new dungeons and a much faster Another Dungeon walk.

**This is a new app (id `anothermacro.app`).** It installs next to 1.x instead of updating it. Export your custom macros from the old app first, then in the new app turn on taps, screen capture, the floating buttons and notifications again, make new connector tokens (System → Claude) and set your macro options and daily schedule again. Statistics start empty. Uninstall the old app when the new one works.

### Macros
- **Sea of Ice** and **Underworld (Demon's Den)** dungeon macros; faster Burning Beast King's Castle and Dogma Tower runs (minimap position reading, battles caught mid-walk, quicker battle commands and end screens).
- Dungeon options: one switch per key colour, runs as a number with an "All keys" switch, party picked by number (basic macro `pick-party`), one switch per collect type (sparkles, fish, jars).
- Phantom Crystal Dimension: late chest banners, blue chests, stops in the World's Cape before the way out.
- Cat scratchbook: routes for more cats (islands, Gadaro, Zarbo, Laula Dome, Purgatory, Nilva, the ??? era tab); missed cats are listed.
- New `inbox` macro (part of the daily routine) and basic `open-menu-page`.
- `watch-ad` copes with commercials that open Google Play, won't close, or never play.
- Macros take over the options of the macros they run (e.g. every dungeon has its own `battle.skill_1`).

### App
- Statistics page: every run, totals and graphs.
- Outcome message at the end of a macro (switch in System).
- "How it works" lists the real steps, with inner macros opened up.
- System → Updates: follow main or an open pull request's test build.
- Logic packs: most fixes reach the phone without an install (dev builds).
- New icons and colours in the logo's style; spinning ring on the floating handle while a macro runs; floating buttons close with the app.
- Always-on: phone news, notifications, a daily schedule, deferred Allow/Deny.

### Under the hood
- The generic core (shell, macro runner, phone connector, relay) is now the separate **macro-forge** project; this app adds the game's profile (`game.yaml`), plugin, macros and art.
- The Termux command-line tool is gone: the app and its connector are the only way to run.

Install: download `another-macro.apk` below.
