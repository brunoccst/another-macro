First official release of Another Macro: an Android app that plays Another Eden for you by reading the screen.

### Macros

**Daily**
- `daily-routine`: start the game, watch the daily ads, the shop ad, claim the cat scratchbook, close the game.
- `start-game`, `daily-ads`, `shop-ad`, `cat-scratchbook`, `stop-game` on their own.

**Another Dungeon**
- `dogma-tower`: walks Dogma Tower, opens the chests, fights on auto, beats the boss and writes a run report. Loops 1 to 10 runs or "all" until the chosen key runs out (it never buys keys).
- `phantom-crystal`: the Phantom Crystal Dimension after a White Key drops. Stops so you can pick the Sensationalist's free Treatise/Codex/Opus yourself.

**Farming**
- `grind-battles`: fights battles on auto (Verdanta on her last skill by default).

### Safety
- Never spends premium currency or confirms a purchase (purchase guard on every tap).

### Known limits
- **Cat Express Ticket Skip has not been tested live yet.** The ticket count reading was fixed just before this release; the first Skip runs will confirm it.
- Not yet tested live: the frozen-game popup handling, the shop ad's own watch path, and the saved place book.
- Dungeon movement still opens the map to find its way; fixed swipe routes are planned.

Install: download `another-macro.apk` below. The app updates itself from new releases.
