First official release of Another Macro: an Android app that plays Another Eden for you by reading the screen. No PC, no root, no Termux.

### Macros

Macros download from the GitHub project and update by themselves, without an app update.

**Daily**
- `daily-routine`: start the game, watch the 5 daily ad chests and the shop ad, the cat scratchbook, claim the inbox, close the game.
- `daily-ads`, `shop-ad`, `cat-scratchbook` (travels to each of today's cats by fixed routes), `inbox` on their own.

**Another Dungeon**
- `dogma-tower` and `burning-beast-kings-castle`: fight each map until "The enemies have disappeared", walk fixed routes to every chest and the exit, beat the boss, write a run report. Loops 1 to 10 runs or "all" until the chosen key runs out (never buys keys); can skip with Cat Express Tickets.
- `phantom-crystal`: the Phantom Crystal Dimension after a White Key drops. Stops so you can pick the free Treatise/Codex/Opus yourself.

**Game and basics**
- `start-game`, `stop-game`; `grind-battles`, `battle` (one skill choice per party slot, used by every macro that fights), `watch-ad`, `open-menu`, `back-to-field`.

### App
- Sidebar with Home, Macros (Daily, Another Dungeon, Farming, Game, Basic, Custom tabs) and System pages; floating start/pause/stop/restart buttons over the game; live log line.
- Claude phone connector: let your own Claude chat play the game and write macros, through a small relay on your own Cloudflare account.

### Safety
- Never spends premium currency or confirms a purchase (purchase guard on every tap; purchase screens are refused).
- Through the connector, anything that can't be undone asks you on the phone first.

Install: download `another-macro.apk` below. Release builds update themselves from new releases.
